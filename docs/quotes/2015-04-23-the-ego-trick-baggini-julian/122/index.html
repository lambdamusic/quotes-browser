The Ego Trick (baggini, Julian) / quote 122 of 254

Much of reality is bundle-like

it’s worth remembering just how much of reality is bundle-like. Atoms are not solid objects but bundles of electrons, neutrons and protons. A plant is a complicated system, not a simple thing. Cars only exist as an assemblage of parts. The internet is a network, not an object that can pinned down in time and space.

Saved 23 April 2015
