Prism and aesthetic provocations

..the end goal of Prism is to produce aesthetic provocations, that is, visualizations which provoke further discussion. But because it is a digital project, Prism can integrate many individual interpretations and create a wide range of visualizations while maintaining data integrity.

Prism expands upon current notions of crowdsourcing to allow for more meaningful interaction with "the crowd." Users interact subjectively with a text and contribute to a collective interpretive energy that has infinite possibilities beyond the highlighting exercise itself

Saved 26 February 2013 / prism.scholarslab.org ↗
