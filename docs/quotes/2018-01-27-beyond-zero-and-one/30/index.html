No cultural frame for psychedelic experience

cultural references for these experiences, except for the counter-cultural movements of the 1960s, but this cultural framework is limited to our distorted memory and history of that time. All these thoughts were floating around in my head when I looked over at a calendar near the bed that featured large photos of fruit and flowers. The images looked so clear and the colors were so intense that I thought I could pull the fruit from the printed paper. I felt at peace, a contentment I had not felt since I was ten years old and waking up and smelling my favorite pillow. I was drained; exhausted.
