The mind as a symbol-manipulating machine

Since Turing’s time, cognitive science has also assumed the mind to be a symbol-manipulating machine, an analogy to the abstract mathematical Turing machine. But as the example of computers that can play chess illustrates, how a computer does math and how a human does math may be two different things entirely.
