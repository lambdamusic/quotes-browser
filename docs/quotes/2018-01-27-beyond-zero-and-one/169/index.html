Sandoz stops sponsoring LSD research

The controversial status of the drug led Sandoz to stop sponsoring clinical trials in 1966. Researchers found it more and more difficult to obtain grants to study LSD. Supplies of the drug for research also became scarce and prohibitively expensive.
