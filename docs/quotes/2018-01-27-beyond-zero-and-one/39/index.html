AI returns to the question of consciousness

Initial excitement about creating machine consciousness in the field’s early days faded when it was realized that creating an artificial mind would be a far greater challenge than was originally believed. AI research has since focused on the development of specialized algorithms and solving technical problems. Only within the last decade has the field again turned to questions about consciousness.
